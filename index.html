Method

Love marriage astrology through Kundli analysis

A planet sitting in the 5th or 7th house doesn't, by itself, prove a love marriage. House lordship, dignity, aspects, conjunctions, dashas and divisional charts all shift the reading — so a proper session looks at how everything works together, not one number in isolation.

The 5th house speaks to romance and attraction; the 7th to partnership and marriage. A meaningful connection between the two is where the real conversation starts.

Love marriage compatibility & Kundli matching

Two people can feel everything for each other and still see career, money, family duty or daily life completely differently. Kundli matching, done properly, isn't a pass/fail test — it's a way to see which parts of a relationship will need more patience and which will come easily.
